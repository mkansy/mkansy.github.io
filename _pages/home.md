I am a **research scientist at [Luma AI](https://lumalabs.ai/)** working on **post-training of large multi-modal models**.
My research interests include various areas of deep learning and especially generative modeling. 
I always find it most fascinating and satisfying if I'm working on projects that interact with the environment in some way, i.e., where I can see/hear/feel its results.
In recent years, I focused mostly on image and video generation and editing tasks.

Previously, I was an **associate research scientist** in the Facial VFX group at **[DisneyResearch\|Studios](https://studios.disneyresearch.com/)** led by [Dr. Derek Bradley](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/~bradleyd/).
From 2021 to 2024, I completed my **PhD** at the **[Computer Graphics Laboratory at ETH Zurich](https://cgl.ethz.ch/)** in cooperation with **[DisneyResearch\|Studios](https://studios.disneyresearch.com/)**, where I was supervised by [Prof. Dr. Markus Gross](https://cgl.ethz.ch/people/grossm/) and [Dr. Romann M. Weber](https://randomwalk.blog/about-me/).

I earned my master’s in *Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence* at the Technical University of Munich, where I developed a strong interest in deep learning in [Prof. Dr. Laura Leal-Taixé's Dynamic Vision and Learning Group](https://dvl.in.tum.de/). Before that, I completed my bachelor’s in *IT-Automotive* at DHBW Stuttgart in collaboration with Robert Bosch GmbH.
